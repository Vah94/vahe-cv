About

I'm Unity Tech Lead and Head of Development at Gora Games, leading the team behind a real-money skill-gaming platform and casual titles on Android and iOS.

Architecture. I design Unity codebases meant to last: clear layer boundaries, dependency injection, MVVM with reactive data flow, Addressables for content, configuration that degrades safely when the network does not cooperate — so several products and a growing team can share one codebase without blocking each other.

Mobile platform. Build size, startup time and memory on real devices, including low-end Android. The SDK layer every mobile game carries: ad mediation, attribution, analytics, consent, push, anti-fraud signals. Store publishing on both platforms.

Team and delivery. CI/CD with tests as a release gate, a planning-first process where every task has a written plan before implementation, and since 2026 AI agents (Claude Code, Codex) as part of everyday work.

Before Gora Games — 6 years at Next Epic, from junior developer to CTO, 40+ shipped casual, merge, match-3 and hypercasual titles.
